This weekend, I have listened an [interview video from bilibili](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1GnQmYxENv/?spm_id_from=333.1007.top_right_bar_window_dynamic.content.click&vd_source=2988d10a40d7656620b16efacc7c7dd2). It is about two graduated phd students talking about whether you should puruse a phd degree or a tenured position.

I strongly aggree with the idea of the 2nd speaker, that "If you treat life like a race and try your best to go to the destination, then what? Your life will be continued. When make your `important` life decisions, donot think this decision will change your life, you own your life, you can change your life. And what the spcific daily life come after this decision is meaningful, not the decision itself."

I think this idea is similar to the "test time scaling" idea in LLM, which if you spend more time and resources when make a decision, you will gain many unique experience and knowledge, whether them 100% useful or not, but they will help you make better decisions in the future.
